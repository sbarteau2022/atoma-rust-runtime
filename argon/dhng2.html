Knowledge distillation through optical refraction

The idea in one line: don't take a model apart to learn what it knows. Shine light through it. Read what it computes, then pass that reading through a lens: a curved medium that bends flat, high-dimensional structure into a space where hierarchy has room. Keep the pattern the light makes, not the model.

The lens, live

This is a real optical medium, not a metaphor. The disk has refractive index n(r) = 2 / (1 − r²), rising without bound toward the rim. Under Fermat's principle, light in this medium follows exactly the straight lines of hyperbolic space. exact maths

Drag inside the disk to move the source. At curvature 0 the rays run straight, as in the teacher's flat space. At 1 they bend into circular arcs meeting the rim at right angles. Rays that started nearly parallel spread apart: that spreading is the room hierarchy needs.

The working theory, in plain language

A large model like Qwen keeps what it knows in internal states: thousands of numbers per word, arranged in a flat space. Flat space is cramped for hierarchy. As a tree branches, the number of branches grows exponentially, but flat space only gives polynomial room, so related ideas get squashed together.

Hyperbolic space grows exponentially, like a tree. So the theory: read the model's states and predictions, and let them refract into a hyperbolic medium, where the branching structure the model learned can spread out and be kept.

The optics make the rule concrete. A lens changes the direction of rays, not what the rays are made of. So we keep the model's relations (what sits near what, what branches from what) and store them as positions and paths in the curved map.

And the model is never cut open. It is read, frozen, and left whole. What gets stored is the refraction pattern, under a hash, where anyone can reproduce it.

How we plan to use it: distillation without tearing models apart

Five stages, each read-only toward the model and each hashed. It mirrors how the carve swarm already works. Specialists are views onto one set of weights: a subset of layers sharing every tensor, healed by small adapters. Nothing is duplicated and the base is never modified.

1 · read

Tap

Qwen 27B stays frozen. We record layer-48 states and its next-op probabilities over the 390 pinned MLIR ops. No weights change.

2 · freeze

Hash

One dump, sharded per kernel variant, every shard hashed. The teacher's floats become fixed bytes.

3 · focus

Project

A measured, lossy step from 5,120 numbers down to a small tangent vector. This is where information is lost, so it gets measured.

4 · refract

Exp map

The tangent vector enters the curved space (H³ × T²) through the exponential map: the lens itself. Integer maths, same bits on every machine.

5 · keep

Map

Positions and relations join the persistent map. Any later model reads the same map, and nothing is lost when the model is swapped.

What is exactly true

  • The disk is an optical medium. Hyperbolic geodesics are light paths for n = 2/(1 − r²). exact maths
  • Ball and hyperboloid are one space. The same distances, reproduced in integer code to 4.4 × 10⁻⁹ across 900 pairs. measured
  • The corpus is pinned. 1,394 unique MLIR variants, all inside the 390-op base, rebuilt byte for byte. measured
  • Carving works on the real model. 12 middle layers removed with no loss; healing took a 48-layer view from 1/10 to 4/10. measured

What the lens cannot do

  • It can't be lossless. One token's state is about 82,000 bits; an H³ × T² point holds 256. Refraction keeps relations, not everything. arithmetic
  • A mirror doesn't reduce dimension. A reflection is an isometry, the same size in and out. The reduction happens in stage 3, and it costs information. exact maths
  • Hosted APIs can't be the teacher. They hide internal states and full probabilities. The teacher must be open weights we run. checked
  • Beauty isn't evidence. A figure that looks stable is not a measured result. see the audit
